# Perla Haddad
@ perla.haddad@example.com | 03 913 077 | Antelias, Lebanon

I have worked in community pharmacy for eight years, the last three as senior pharmacy assistant at Pharmacie Saint Elie in Antelias.

I dispense under the supervision of the pharmacist, manage the counter and handle the things that make a pharmacy run: I do the weekly order to three wholesalers, I run the stock rotation and expiry checks across roughly 4,000 lines, and I handle the cold chain log for vaccines and insulin, including the twice-daily fridge temperature record and what to do when it goes out of range. Controlled drug register entries are mine to keep accurate and I have never had a discrepancy at audit.

I deal with patients all day, which includes the difficult conversations: a prescription that is not covered, a medication that is out of stock nationally, a customer who is upset and is not really upset about the pharmacy. I have trained four new assistants on the till and the dispensing software.

I manage the schedule for the three assistants on my shift pattern, though the pharmacy owner sets the opening hours and does the payroll. I do not see the pharmacy's profit and loss. I know our margins on OTC lines because I do the pricing updates, but I have never been given a cost target.

Before Saint Elie I was a pharmacy assistant at Pharmacie Al Rayan in Dekwaneh for five years, doing the same work with less responsibility.

I hold a Technical Baccalaureate in Pharmacy Assistance, 2016, and a first aid certificate renewed in 2024. Arabic native, English good, French good.
