# Carla Mouawad

I am a marketing coordinator with five years of experience, currently at Levant Beverage Co, where I run social media and campaign coordination for two drink brands across Lebanon, Jordan and the UAE.

My week is mostly content calendar, agency coordination and reporting. I plan and schedule about forty posts a month across Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn, brief our creative agency on assets, and run the approval loop with brand and legal. I manage a paid social budget of around eighteen thousand dollars a month on Meta and TikTok Ads, and I build the monthly performance deck that goes to the marketing director. Our Instagram following grew from 34,000 to 91,000 over two years and I ran the influencer programme that did most of that, which meant negotiating with about sixty creators and being the person who chased them for deliverables.

I do a lot of work in spreadsheets. I pull the campaign data out of Meta Ads Manager and TikTok, clean it, and build the reporting model in Excel with pivot tables and a few lookups. I taught myself enough Google Sheets scripting to automate the weekly refresh so I stopped doing it by hand on Monday mornings. I have used Google Analytics and Looker Studio for dashboards.

Before this I was a marketing assistant at Rawad Retail for two years, doing in-store promotion coordination, print collateral and event support for seasonal campaigns.

I hold a BA in Marketing and Communication from Haigazian University, 2019. I speak Arabic, English and French, and I have a Meta Blueprint certification from 2023.

Skills: social media strategy, content planning, paid social, influencer marketing, Meta Ads Manager, TikTok Ads, Google Analytics, Looker Studio, Excel, Canva, Hootsuite, copywriting in Arabic and English.
