# Sandra Ibrahim
@ sandra.ibrahim@example.com / 71 662 903 / Byblos

I have worked in restaurants since I was nineteen and I have been a supervisor for the last four years, currently at Marina Grill in Byblos, a 120-cover place that is very busy from June to September and very quiet the rest of the year.

What I do there: I run the floor on six shifts a week, usually as the senior person on site in the evening. I have eleven servers and three bar staff reporting to me on a Friday. I write the weekly rota for front of house, which the general manager signs off, and I do the section planning and the reservation book. I train every new server on the floor, which over four years has been about twenty five people, and I wrote the two-page service standards card that we use for it because there was nothing written down before.

I handle complaints myself unless they involve a refund over fifty dollars, in which case the GM decides. During the summer I am the person who deals with the coach parties, which means I have got good at negotiating a set menu with a tour operator and then making the kitchen believe it is possible.

On the numbers: I do the stock count for the bar every Sunday and I know our pour cost, and I flag it when it drifts. But I do not own the P&L. I have never been given the food cost target or held to it, the GM handles supplier pricing and I am not in those conversations, and I have not seen a full monthly P&L for the site. I want a role where I do own that, which is why I am applying, but I would be doing it for the first time.

I hold a Food Safety Level 2 certificate, renewed last year. I have not been the responsible person during an inspection, though I have been on site for two.

I did two years of a hospitality diploma at a technical institute in Jbeil and did not finish it; I took a full-time job instead. Arabic native, English fluent, French basic.
